# LaunchLint v0.3 — Pricing Context

## Role of PDF Reports
PDF reports are the primary upsell trigger for agency/client use cases. They make LaunchLint deliverable to non-technical stakeholders.

## Packaging
- **Core CLI**: free forever; includes text + JSON output.
- **PDF Reports**: included in v0.3 base package to drive adoption; gating considered for v0.4+ once usage data exists.
- **White-label**: available via CLI flags and brand config; no separate tier at launch.

## Upsell Path
- v0.3: agencies can generate branded reports → word-of-mouth.
- Future: batch/multi-page reports, scheduled audits, team dashboard.

## Pricing Principles
- Keep friction low: one package, all features.
- Monetize on scale (volume, teams) not on a single feature.
- PDF reports justify a higher price ceiling when a paid tier is introduced later.
